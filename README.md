# 🎓 ESCROW-ED

> **A Secure, Student-Centric Micro-Task Marketplace Powered by Escrow**

ESCROW-ED is a platform designed exclusively for university students to safely earn, learn, and collaborate through campus-based micro-tasks. By integrating an **Escrow Hold-and-Release** payment model, verified student authentication, and AI-powered security, ESCROW-ED creates a trusted ecosystem where students can exchange skills without worrying about fraud.

---

## 🚀 Problem Statement

Traditional freelance platforms are not built for students.

* ❌ High competition from experienced professionals
* ❌ No campus-specific marketplace
* ❌ Payment scams and trust issues
* ❌ Lack of opportunities for beginners

ESCROW-ED addresses these challenges with a secure, student-first solution.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. Student posts a task.
2. Another student accepts it.
3. Payment is placed into Escrow.
4. Task is completed and verified.
5. Escrow releases payment securely.

This workflow ensures fairness and eliminates payment-related fraud.
